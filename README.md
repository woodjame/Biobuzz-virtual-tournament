# BIOBUZZ Virtual Tournament – Largo Robotics

### ▶ Tournament site: **https://woodjame.github.io/Biobuzz-tournament/**

Practice and test on the main simulator: **https://woodjame.github.io/Biobuzz-auto-simulation/** (AUTO, TeleOp, full match, Records).

This site is only for **virtual tournaments**. It's the full match simulator with the **Tournament** box, kept separate so the main simulator never changes when the tournament does. It runs in Chrome, Edge or Firefox on a laptop, with your controller (PlayStation, Xbox, REV, Logitech).

> **Your robot card here is separate from the main site.** Browsers keep each site's settings apart, so set up your card again on this site: team number and name, controller and buttons, and upload your code – **AUTO and TeleOp** for full matches. Your code runs only on your laptop: in TeleOp just your robot's commands are sent, and for AUTO just its moves (drive here, intake, launch…) – never the code itself.

The **tournament site** runs a bracket online: alliances of 2 teams play each other from anywhere in the world. One laptop is the **director** – it runs the field for every match, so everyone sees the same field and the scores come straight back to the director. Every team drives from its own laptop with its own controller, buttons and code.

## Match type: full match or TeleOp only

The director chooses, and can change it between any two matches:
- **Full match** – 2:30 like the real field: **AUTO** (0:30) from each robot's own AUTO code, the 8-second transition ("Drivers, pick up your controllers"), then **TeleOp** (2:00).
- **TeleOp only** – 2:00 TeleOp. Both alliances start at 0 with the field as it looks after a typical AUTO.

**AUTO for either alliance.** You don't know which side you'll be called to, so upload **both** your Red and your Blue AUTO (with your helper files) in one upload. When you're called, the simulator uses the AUTO whose file name or OpMode name says **Red** or **Blue** to match your alliance. Your robot starts where your AUTO says – agree on starting spots with your alliance partner. The director sees a warning if an AUTO starts on the other alliance's side, or in the same spot as its partner's.

## Views: 3-D, split screen
- **Watchers, the MC and the broadcast screen** see the match in **3-D, split screen**: the **red alliance on the left half** (from behind the red drivers' wall) and the **blue alliance on the right half** (from behind the blue drivers' wall). Each half has its own camera buttons – **Driver view, Audience, Overhead, Hive, Ride along** (pick any robot) – and dragging, panning or zooming on a half moves only that half. Every laptop chooses its own views. **Single view** goes back to one full-size view (your laptop remembers it); **Split screen** brings the split back. On the broadcast screen the camera buttons fade out until you point at them.
- **Teams** see **one full-size 3-D view from their own alliance's drivers' side** when they're first called to the field – change it any time with the camera buttons (or **2-D view**).

## Your robot, your code, your buttons
Everything on your robot card goes with you to the field: your **AUTO code** (Red and Blue), your **TeleOp code**, your robot settings (drivetrain, speeds, weight, shooter, camera, servos) and your **controller buttons** for the driver and operator. The director's laptop runs your robot with your settings; your code itself stays on your laptop.

## The score during a match

Like a real event, the alliance totals stay hidden from the teams, the watchers and the stream until the right moment:
- **TeleOp only:** no totals during the match; the **final score** shows at the end.
- **Full match:** no totals during AUTO; **both AUTO scores** show when AUTO ends (a big "AUTO score" card on the stream, then "AUTO SCORE" along the top during TeleOp); the **final score** shows at the end.

The director and the MC see the live score the whole time.

## Director (the person running the tournament)
1. Use the laptop with the best internet, plugged in, with the tournament site in front. Press **Tournament → Create a tournament** (give it a name, and tick **Full matches** if you want AUTO). You get a 6-letter **tournament code** and four links: **Copy team link**, **Copy watch link**, **Copy broadcast link**, **Copy MC link**. Send teams the code or the team link.
2. Teams appear in the **Waiting room** as they check in. Type the **alliances** – 2 teams each, best seed first (seed 1 at the top; use ↑ to reorder). A team number in **green** has checked in. Tick **Final is best of 3** if you want that, or **Shuffle the seeds** for a random draw. Press **Make the bracket**. With 3, 5, 6 or 7 alliances the top seeds get a **bye**; the better seed is always **red**.
3. The next match is picked for you (click another match in the bracket to play it first). Choose the **Match type**. Press **Call these teams to the field** – each team's page joins the field by itself, in its starting spot (**⇄ swap spots** first if an alliance wants the other spots). Everyone can drive around until you start. In a full match you also see whether each robot's **AUTO code** is ready.
4. Press **Start the full match** or **Start the 2:00 TeleOp match**. When it ends, the final score appears for you: **Confirm** it, **Replay** it (it doesn't count), or pick the winner yourself (for a tie, or a foul you want to call). The winner moves on, and the teams go back to the waiting room. In a best-of-3 final, the teams stay on the field for the next game.
5. A team that doesn't show up: **Red advances (no-show)** / **Blue advances (no-show)**. **Announce** sends a message to everyone (e.g. "Semifinals start in 5 minutes"); it shows on the stream for 15 seconds.
6. **Announcers:** every MC who joins is listed. Tick **on air** for the MCs calling the next match – only on-air MCs are heard on the stream, so each match can have its own MC (or two). **Computer voice calls the plays** makes the broadcast screen read out hive tips, fouls and the AUTO and final scores when no MC is on air.
7. The tournament is saved on the director's laptop. If the page reloads or closes, open the tournament site again and press **Tournament → Reopen "…"**: the same code opens again and teams, watchers and MCs reconnect by themselves within a minute or two.

## Teams
1. Open the tournament site and set up your robot card first: **team number and team name**, your controller and buttons, and your code – **AUTO (Red and Blue) and TeleOp** for full matches, TeleOp for TeleOp-only matches.
2. Press **Tournament** (it opens by itself), type the code (or open the team link), and press **Join the waiting room**. You see the bracket, who's checked in and your next match.
3. **Keep the page open and in front.** When your match is called, the field opens by itself ("You're called to the field") and you're placed in your starting spot. After the match you go back to the waiting room.

## MC – calling the play-by-play
1. Press **Tournament → Announce (MC)** (or open the MC link), type the code and **the name to show on the stream**, and press **Join as the MC**.
2. The **MC panel** floats over the field: the match, both alliances and every robot's team, the **live score** and clock, and every play as it happens (tips, scores, fouls) with calling tips. Watch in 2-D or 3-D.
3. Press **Turn on my microphone** (allow the microphone when the browser asks). **Use headphones**, so the stream doesn't echo. When the director puts you **on air**, your voice goes straight to every broadcast screen – into the stream – and your name shows on it (🎙). **Mute** any time; the level bar shows the stream can hear you.
4. Build the suspense: the stream hides the totals until the end of AUTO (full match) and the end of the match – you can tease the score and then read the final.

## Watching and streaming
- Anyone can press **Tournament → Watch** with the code (or open the watch link) to watch every match live, in 2-D or 3-D.
- **Broadcast screen** (or the broadcast link) is a clean full-screen view for streaming with OBS to YouTube or Twitch: alliance names and teams along the top, the score by the rules above, the big AUTO and final score cards, announcements, the on-air MC's name, and the bracket between matches. It plays the on-air MC's voice – in OBS, capture the browser window and its audio. If the browser blocks the sound, a **Click to hear the MC** button appears. Choose the 3-D view first if you want it; **Esc** leaves the broadcast screen. Use a separate laptop for the broadcast screen, not the director's.

Tournament matches don't go on the Records page and send only team numbers and names – never code. Up to 16 alliances. The same tips as **Play online** apply: if laptops can't connect, try a phone hotspot or home Wi-Fi; everyone needs the same version of the page (Ctrl+Shift+R); teams far from the director see a little more delay.
